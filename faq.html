Faq's

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does an interior project take?

Project timelines depend on the size and design requirements, but most interiors are completed within a few weeks.

Q: Do you provide customized interior designs?

Yes, we create personalized interiors based on your space, style, and budget preferences.

Q: What materials do you use for interiors?

We use high-quality plywood, laminates, premium finishes, and durable hardware for long-lasting interiors.

Q: Can I get a quote before starting the project?

Yes, we provide an estimated quotation based on your requirements and selected interior package.

Q: Do you handle modular kitchen designs?

Yes, we design modern modular kitchens with smart storage and functional layouts.

Q: What is included in your interior package?

Our packages may include design, materials, wardrobes, modular kitchen, TV units, and installation services.

Q: Do you offer site visits and consultations?

Yes, we provide site visits and expert consultations to understand your space and requirements better.

Q: How do I start my interior project with you?

Simply contact our team, share your requirements, and we will guide you through the complete process.
